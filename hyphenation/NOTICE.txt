The site bundles these unmodified patterns as a string in ru-hyphenation.js
and applies Liang's pattern-matching algorithm. It inserts discretionary
soft hyphens in the mobile reading view. Original chapter data and downloads
are unchanged. The unmodified pattern source is provided alongside the asset.
